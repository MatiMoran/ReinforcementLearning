# Notes

[1](part0025_split_002.html#fn1x18-bk)As we mentioned in Section [6.1](part0014_split_001.html#sec1-44), *δ**t* in our notation is defined to be *R**t*+1 +*γV*(*S**t*+1)−*V* (*S**t*), so *δ**t* is not available until time *t*+1. The TD error *available* at *t* is actually *δ**t*−1 = *R**t*+*γV*(*S**t*)−*V* (*S**t*−1). Because we are thinking of time steps as very small, or even infinitesimal, time intervals, one should not attribute undue importance to this one-step time shift.

[2](part0025_split_003.html#fn2x18-bk)In the literature relating TD errors to the activity of dopamine neurons, their *δ**t* is the same as our *δ**t*−1 = *R**t* + *γV*(*S**t*) − *V* (*S**t*−1).
